Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation
m0: 23,260 kg mf: 460 kg Isp: 320 s Δv: 150 m/s

Derived by integrating Newton's second law for variable mass systems: \( m\frac{dv}{dt} = -v_e\frac{dm}{dt} \). Integrating from \( t_0 \) to \( t_f \) yields \( \Delta v = v_e \ln\frac{m_0}{m_f} \), where \( v_e = I_{\text{sp}} g_0 \).

Vis-Viva Orbital Speed
μ: 3.986×10¹⁴ m³/s² r: 6,871 km v_orb: 7.613 km/s

Conservation of specific mechanical energy: \( \epsilon = \frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r} = -\frac{\mu}{2a} \). Solving for velocity gives the Vis-Viva relation.

Kepler's Third Law (Orbital Period)
a: 6,871 km T_orb: 94.6 min

Equating gravitational force with centripetal acceleration for circular orbits: \( \frac{\mu}{r^2} = \omega^2 r \). Since \( \omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} \), it follows that \( T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2 a^3}{\mu} \).

Hohmann Transfer Burns

Impulsive co-planar transfer between two circular orbits. The semi-major axis of the transfer ellipse is \( a_{\text{trans}} = \frac{r_1 + r_2}{2} \).
